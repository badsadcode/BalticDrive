# Gas pump

`gas_pump.glb`: a petrol pump (dispenser), modelled by Tom (Bad Ideas Works) in Blender, 22 Nov 2026, a work in
progress he means to improve. The studio's own work, part of Baltic Drive. One mesh, four flat colour materials (no
textures): dark details, grey body, red top, dark base. Modelled about 1.44 × 4.32 × 2.93 m, origin at the bottom
centre, +Y up, its long side along Z; the game scales it to about 2.3 m tall and stands it on its own concrete island
(D126).
