# Generator

`generator.glb`: a portable petrol generator, modelled by Tom (Bad Ideas Works) in Blender, 21 Nov 2026. The studio's
own work, part of Baltic Drive. One mesh, seven flat colour materials (no textures); about 0.99 × 0.83 × 0.76 m,
origin at the bottom centre, +Y up. Material 0 ("Material", red) is the tank. For D108 (power without the grid).
