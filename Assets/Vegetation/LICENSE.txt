Thornwake Woodland vegetation pack (17 trees, 3 levels of detail each, and Canopy.gdshader).

Made for Thornwake, a Bad Ideas Works project, and owned by Tom (github.com/badsadcode). Used in Baltic Drive
with his permission (13 November 2026). The meshes are original low-poly models and the textures are original
image-generated maps; see README_Thornwake.md. Not for use outside Bad Ideas Works projects without asking Tom.
