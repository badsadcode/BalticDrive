# Mixing Noor's concepts: Sage's brainstorm (not canon)

Written 21 Nov 2026 after reading [Noor's concepts](APOCALYPSE_STORY_CONCEPTS.md). Noor and Sage, asked separately,
both landed near the same place (Noor: Eleven Days + Evacuation; Sage, before reading hers: a blackout, with NPCs who
disagree about why). Below, one way to grow that into a shape that expands with the map.

## Three acts that follow the geography

1. **The Eleven Days** — the past, told only in fragments. The grid fails on a Tuesday at 09:17; phones, fuel and shops
   follow; the last broadcast ends mid-sentence on day 11. Nobody agrees why (Noor's "conflicting testimony"): every
   survivor has a theory. It fits what the game already is: old simple cars still run, fuel and batteries are
   precious, radios matter (the transistor radio already in the drawers becomes a key item).
2. **The Evacuation** — the mystery, everywhere on the map. Convoys went west and north; some never arrived. It is the
   reason for the campaign (the player's friends were evacuated to different places, so the player crosses Europe to
   find them) and the material for generated quests at real places: reception centres in real schools with passenger
   lists, convoys of wrecks on main roads (the trucks on the shoulders, D101, already read as one), a name on a manifest.
3. **The Other Side** — the late game, as the map reaches the sea. Radios pick up live voices from beyond the Baltic:
   weather reports, shipping, ordinary talk. Did the world end, or carry on without us? It pulls the player to the
   ports and ferries, the Baltic lap and the UK. Noor's principle 7 ("keep the geography expandable") becomes the plot.

## The Warsaw lights

At night some empty Warsaw districts briefly light up: a mystery the player can **see** from far away. It needs no
answer, draws the curious (and bandits), and is cheap to make (lit windows are a material switch at night).

## Borrowed, small

* **The Last Summer** — its grounded tone as everyday life years later: communities, trade, farming, road pirates.
* **Black Rain** — only a rumour ("don't drink from the rivers"); perhaps water purification one day.
* **The Signal** — one more theory among survivors ("there was a tone on every radio before it started…"), never
  confirmed.

## The Last Ordinary Tuesday, cheaply

One street at morning rush hour, in the player's car, the radio on; the traffic lights go out. Cut to "Day 312": the same
street, empty, the car under leaves. Thirty seconds, no city simulation.

## How the game could tell it (systems that exist or are near)

Notes and documents as loot (D89 tables); radio broadcasts on the transistor radio; NPCs with their own versions and
small quests at real addresses; abandoned convoys from the wreck and parked-car fields (D99–D101); lit districts at night.
